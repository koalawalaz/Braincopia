Trick 01 / 29 — Voice

Vary your sentence lengths on purpose

Models write at a steady cadence — most sentences land between 15 and 25 words, forever. Humans don't. We run long when we're building an argument, then stop. Hard. Go through your draft and force one sentence under five words into every paragraph. That single edit breaks the metronome that makes text read as machine-written.

Tell 01

The tricolon habit

Three items. Always three. Models reach for the rule of three so reliably that a page with four lists of three reads as generated before anyone finishes it.

Tell 02

The throat-clearing open

"In today's rapidly evolving landscape." Nobody talks like this. Cut every sentence before the first real claim and the piece starts sounding like a person again.

Tell 03

The untouched template

Default fonts, default spacing, default stock. A layout nobody adjusted reads as low effort before a single word is processed — and it happens in Canva more than anywhere else.

Tell 04

Abstraction with no object

"Meaningful engagement." "Significant implications." Swap any abstract noun for one concrete detail only you would know, and the sentence becomes unfakeable.
