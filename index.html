The Five Laws

What the magazine is arguing, in five lines

01

AI is not creative. You are.

The model recombines. The judgement about what deserves to exist stays with the person holding the keyboard.

02

The tool is never the tell.

Nobody can see which software you opened. They can see whether you made a decision inside it.

03

Specific beats impressive.

One concrete detail only you would know outperforms any sentence engineered to sound significant.

04

Rhythm carries the person.

Meaning survives paraphrase. Cadence does not. It is the part of your writing a model cannot borrow.

05

We do not gatekeep perspectives.

We publish them. A view nobody else holds is the only real competitive advantage left.
