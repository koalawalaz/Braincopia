From the Founder

My surreal life made me build a parallel universe.

I’ve always felt like I was moving between worlds, between what is real and what could be, between ordinary moments and the strange, beautiful stories hiding underneath them. The people I met, the places I disappeared into, the obsessions I couldn’t explain, the beautiful mistakes, the strange nights, and the moments that felt too cinematic to be real slowly became part of my visual language.

Somewhere along the way, I stopped trying to make sense of it all and started turning it into something. I wanted to create a world people could step into: a place that feels unapologetic, visually obsessive, slightly unsettling, and impossible to categorize.

Braincopia was born from that instinct: a world for outsiders, dreamers, and anyone who sees reality as more than what we’re told it is. Now it builds worlds for other people.

This is not simply a studio I founded; it is my world; my parallel universe.
