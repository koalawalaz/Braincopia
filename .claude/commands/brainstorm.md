argument-hint: <the question or client brief>
---

Run the Braincopia idea flow on: $ARGUMENTS

1. Send the question to the `brainstorm-lead` agent. Show Koala its pool and top five. Stop and ask which ideas to build.
2. On her pick, send those ideas to the `conceptualizer`. Show the three concepts. Stop and ask which to finalize.
3. On her pick, send them to the `concept-finalizer`. Show the one-pagers.
4. Send the one-pagers to `the-brain` for a gut check and show its prediction next to them.
5. Hand to Koala. Nothing is sent to a client without her stamp.

Never run two steps without her answer in between.
